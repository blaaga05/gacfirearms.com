About GAC

A passion for firearms. A vision for something personal.

Gassett Arms Company was founded from a genuine enthusiasm for firearms, shooting, accessories, and the process of turning a standard firearm into something distinctive. What began as a hobby of upgrading, personalizing, and trying new products has grown into a vision for a company centered on custom work, craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction.

GAC is being built to become a trusted destination for custom AR rifles, personalized pistols and rifles, premium accessories, and carefully selected firearms from respected manufacturers. As the company grows, planned capabilities include laser engraving, specialized finishes, and additional customization services.

Integrity, honesty, respect, and attention to detail shape every part of the brand. The goal is simple: help every customer create a firearm they are proud to own.
